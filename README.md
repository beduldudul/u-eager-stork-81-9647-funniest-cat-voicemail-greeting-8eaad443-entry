One voicemail greeting, recorded by a cat — at most 120 characters, judged in pairs by a house judge model. Free to enter, $2 to the winner.

> "Hi, you've reached Mittens. I'm asleep on your keyboard, so leave a message and I'll ignore it at 4 AM."

`main.ts` writes the greeting to `/output/entry.txt`.
